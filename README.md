# StayNest_Hotel_Bookings-
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 5,000 hotel bookings (2024–2026): revenue by hotel, country, channel and month. Cleaned with Excel &amp; Power Query. Topics: power-bi, data-analysis, data-visualization, power-query, excel, business-intelligence, dashboard, kaggle
🏨 Hotel Bookings Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes 5,000 bookings across five StayNest hotels (January 2024 – July 2026). It shows how much revenue each hotel earns, how the average booking value moves through the year, and how reservations split across booking channels.

Dashboard preview

🎯 Business Questions
How much revenue, how many bookings and how many guests does the group generate?
Which hotels contribute the most revenue?
How does the average revenue per booking change across the months?
How are reservations split across booking channels and countries?
📂 Dataset
Source: Kaggle – StayNest Hotel Bookings
Size: 5,000 bookings, January 2024 to July 2026
Key fields: Booking_ID, Hotel_Name, Country, Date, Guests, Booking_Channel, Room_Rate_USD, Total_Revenue_USD
🛠️ Tools

Excel · Power Query · Power BI

🧹 Data Preparation
Handled missing values (nulls)
Removed duplicate records
Corrected data types (dates, numbers, text)
Cleaned in Excel and Power Query before loading into Power BI

Visuals: KPI cards, average revenue per booking by month (line), revenue by country (pie), revenue by hotel (bar), bookings by channel (donut), and slicers for country and month.

Note: the country chart displays five countries (Australia, Canada, Spain, United Kingdom, Japan) and the channel chart displays three channels (Booking.com, Direct Website, Airbnb). Their percentages are shares of the items shown, not of the full dataset.

🔍 Key Insights
Hotel revenue is evenly spread. All five hotels earn between roughly $0.63M and $0.69M. StayNest Downtown leads and StayNest Business is lowest, a gap of about 9%.
Average booking value is stable through the year. It stays between about $640 and $690 per booking, peaking in May and dipping in June, a swing of roughly ±3%.
Countries are close to equal. Among the five countries displayed, each contributes about $0.33M–$0.34M in revenue (19–20% of the five).
No single channel dominates. Booking.com (about 1.05K bookings), Direct Website (about 1.03K) and Airbnb (about 0.98K) each bring in roughly a third of the bookings shown.
